## 1.0.0 (2026-11-19)
- First version of the Ember & Oak concept site.
- WebGL ember hero with a still fallback, plus scroll-driven parallax and reveals.
- Live menu with dietary filters and search, and a tasting menu builder with wine pairings.
- Reservation form with time slots, validation and a confirmation dialog (demo only, nothing is sent).
- Live open or closed badge, illustrated gallery with lightbox, and light and dark themes.
- Lighthouse 100 for performance, accessibility, best practices and SEO on mobile and desktop. Page weight 226 KB including fonts, 0 accessibility violations (axe) in light and dark, 62 automated tests passing.
