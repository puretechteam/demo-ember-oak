01Our story

We built the kitchen around the fire.

Ember & Oak opened in 2019 in an old blacksmith's shop on Cinder Lane. The forge is gone, but the chimney stayed, and so did the idea behind it: cook everything over wood and let the fire set the pace.

Chef Mateo Arana spent eight years on hearths in the Basque Country and the Bay Area before coming home to Oregon. His menu moves with the coals. It is hot and fast at five for flatbreads and clams, and low and slow by nine for the cuts that have been resting at the back of the grate all afternoon.

We burn Oregon white oak split at a family mill in Yamhill County, with cherry and apple prunings from nearby orchards when spring comes around.
